# Nathan Osei: commercial calendar
Mon, 22 Sept 25

Nathan serves as Store Operations Manager at Vanguard Champs-Élysées, operating as a franchise under Levant Group.

### Difficulties with the current M7 system
- M7 remains the sole HRS module that has not been migrated to a modern, user-friendly interface
- The interface is extremely cumbersome, posing challenges even for seasoned managers
	- Very little documentation or training material is available
	- Learning the system largely depends on trial and error
	- Even returning users find themselves re-reading the manual every year
- Setting up prayer-time exceptions is particularly problematic
	- There is no straightforward way to handle mid-day closures
	- Managers frequently need to wipe the entire annual calendar and rebuild it from scratch
	- The system does not always apply the configured exceptions as expected
### Commercial calendar workflow and needs
- How scheduling information is gathered:
	- The mall management team distributes operating hour requirements via official email
	- The back office processes and enters the updates
	- A yearly calendar is then built inside M7
- Three main scheduling scenarios must be supported:
	- Standard opening hours (set once and applied throughout the year)
	- Seasonal adjustments for local religious observance (e.g. 10AM-4PM, then 7PM-12AM)
	- One-off events with special hours (e.g. Black Friday: round-the-clock operations at the Champs-Élysées location)
- Advance notice for special events is typically only two to three weeks
- The calendar generally requires updates twice per year: at the start of the year and before the religious observance season
- The existing system offers no way to modify entries in place; managers must delete and recreate them
- Nathan developed AI-assisted mockup designs proposing a more intuitive interface
- Key capabilities requested for a new tool:
	- Manager authentication codes for added security (modelled on the stock management system)
	- A live status indicator showing current state (e.g. "Currently open until 9PM")
	- The ability to plan schedules on a month-by-month basis
	- Automatic detection of conflicting or overlapping time slots
	- Period-based scheduling support (e.g. Jan-Mar standard hours, April religious observance, May-Dec standard hours)
