# Margaret: commercial calendar
Thu, 18 Sept 25

Margaret works within the HR team and handles personnel administration for Vanguard stores across the Netherlands.

### Commercial calendar - Overview and workflows
- Margaret maintains the commercial calendar for the Netherlands by preparing a shared Excel file on an annual basis
	- The file covers bank holidays across multiple jurisdictions (Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg)
	- Holiday data is sourced directly from government publications at the start of each fiscal year
- HR validates the calendar before it is distributed to stores
- Final communication goes out by email in November for the upcoming year
### Opening hours and bank holidays
- Bank holidays are populated automatically in the M7 system (loaded centrally by a dedicated team)
- Specific opening hours are shared with stores well ahead of time
	- Margaret begins working on Christmas schedules as early as September
	- Shopping centre coordination typically happens around three months in advance
- Every Vanguard store in the Netherlands remains open on bank holidays with adjusted hours
	- Historical exception: Kalverstraat used to close on King's Day due to extreme congestion in the area
	- Currently the network consists mainly of shopping centre locations plus two high-street stores (Kalverstraat and Eindhoven)
### Current and future collaborative tools
- Margaret relies on a shared Excel workbook with real-time editing alongside the structure team
	- Access is managed through a shared link with differentiated permissions (view-only vs. edit)
	- The workbook tracks weekly staff placement, team holidays, general manager schedules, and more
